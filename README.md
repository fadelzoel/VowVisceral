## Quick Summary
So basically this mod force you into class selection where you cannot use other weapon that is not your class selection. If you did use a mage weapon while in Melee Class,
the projectile damage of that weapon will be 1, though it may not be always 1 since there are other factor like effect, etc.
You can reset your class selection using Scroll, though using one shall reset your entire progress
(NOTE: PROGRESS CANNOT BE EASILY GAINED EVEN IF YOU HAVE END GAME ARMOR)

EXP Mechanic: There will be experience mechanic that is unique to your player only, gain experience from killing mobs. It may not be balanced right now, but I'm working on it.

Level: Once you reach certain level, you will be granted permanent buff based on your class selection.
        Example> If you are using True Melee, you will be granted a little bit of life steal at the level of 20 

Vow: There will be certain point where your progression hit a certain threshold, which mean you will be eligible to do a quest to progress your class further and achieve more buffs/skills.
