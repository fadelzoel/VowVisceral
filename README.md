## What is this mod actually doing?

So basically, this mod forces you into a hard **Class Selection** where you literally cannot use weapons outside your chosen path. 

If you try to flex a Mage weapon while locked into Melee Class? Enjoy your **1 damage** projectile. (Well, maybe slightly more than 1 depending on weird item effects).
Why do you even want to use Aetherflux Cannon when all you do is spam Murasama, you weirdo.

* **The Reset Penalty:** You can reset your class selection using a Scroll. However, using one will **reset your entire progression**.
  * ⚠️ **WARNING:** Progress cannot be easily regained, even if you are wearing literal endgame armor. You have been warned.

## 🧠 Mechanics Overview

### 1. Unique EXP System
* A custom experience mechanic tied directly to your player.
* Gain EXP by killing mobs.
* *Is it balanced right now?* Hell naw, but I'm working on it.

### 2. Leveling & Permanent Buffs
* Hitting specific level milestones grants permanent, class-tailored buffs.
* *Example:* Reaching Level 20 on a **True Melee** build grants built-in lifesteal.

### 3. The Vow (Class Quests)
* Hit a progression threshold -> Become eligible for a **Vow Quest**.
* Completing Vows unlocks deeper class progression, higher stat caps, and unique active/passive skills.
